About

Hello, I'm Mirza Tairin.

I am an MSc student in Computer Science at BRAC University and a software developer with a background in database and enterprise application development.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Human-Computer Interaction, Explainable AI, and Responsible AI.

I am particularly interested in understanding how intelligent systems reason, how people interact with them, and how we can build AI systems that are more interpretable, reliable, and centered around human needs.

Outside of computing, I enjoy singing, digital art, photography, literature, manga, and video games.

Language Learning

My motivation for learning different languages comes primarily from my enthusiasm for games, comics, music, and literature from around the world. I enjoy practicing Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and French through Duolingo, and I achieved an 8.0 in the IELTS Academic examination because I wanted to become more confident in expressing myself in English.

While I deeply appreciate the immense effort translators put into making media from around the world accessible, I believe that some subtle meanings, cultural nuances, and expressions can inevitably be lost in translation. For this reason, I enjoy experiencing the original versions alongside translations whenever possible, whether I am reading my favorite manga and literature or exploring song lyrics.
